    Process & warranty

    One crew. Zero hand-offs.

    Here's how a Barrier Reef shell gets from the factory to your backyard, who does each step, and exactly what's covered afterwards.

    Warranty, explained

    Two warranties. Both in writing.

    The manufacturer covers the shell. iTop covers the work. Here's who to call for what.

    Manufacturer · Barrier Reef

    The shell

    Lifetime Limited Structural Warranty

    The shell stays structurally sound, meaning capable of containing water, for the life of the pool. Transferable once if you sell your home.

    15-Year Finish Warranty

    Covers the gelcoat finish against blistering.

    Summary only. The written Barrier Reef warranty document governs, and it covers the pool shell only.

    Installer · iTop

    The workmanship

    Installation & plumbing

    Excavation, leveling, backfill, plumbing and equipment set by our own crew. [iTop to confirm term]

    Decking & pavers

    Paver, travertine and porcelain decks laid by the same team. [iTop to confirm term]

    Equipment

    Pumps, heaters and automation carry their manufacturers' warranties. We register them for you.

    You

    Keeping it valid

    Keep water balanced

    Finish warranties assume water chemistry stays in range. We show you how at handover.

    Never drain it yourself

    Groundwater can push an empty shell upward. Always call us first.

    Keep your paperwork

    We hand you the serial number, warranty registration and as-built drawing.

    Why Barrier Reef

    Shells built in a factory, not your backyard.

    A fiberglass shell is molded and quality-checked in controlled conditions, then delivered in one piece. That's why it goes in fast and stays smooth.
