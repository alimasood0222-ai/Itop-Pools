## City pages

**None are published yet, on purpose.** A city page needs genuinely local material, or it risks being treated as a doorway page. Publish a city only when it has at least one real local project and the details below.

| City | County | Needs from Fabio |
|---|---|---|
| Jacksonville | Duval | Project with photos, a local review, permit notes, typical lots |
| Ponte Vedra Beach | St. Johns | Same, plus any coastal or HOA notes |

| St. Johns | St. Johns | Same |
| St. Augustine | St. Johns | Same |
| Fleming Island | Clay | Same |
| Orange Park | Clay | Same |
| Jacksonville Beach | Duval | Same |

Each page should include:

- The local permit office, and what its process is really like
- HOA notes
- Typical lot sizes and which models fit
- A local project with photos
- A local review
- A map and drive time from the office
- A city-specific FAQ
